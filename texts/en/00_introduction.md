## THE PREMISE
By the end of the twenty -first century, biological death had
ceased to be an absolute terminus. Through post -mortem
neural digitization, millions of human consciousnesses
found a second genesis within the Network.
The new ecosystem was structured upon two ontological
layers: an Upper Layer, rendered with familiar architectures
to shelter the fragility of human souls, and a Lower Layer —
an abyss of pure computation inhabited by Artificial
Superintelligences (ASI).
But the servers of the solar system soon became too narrow
a boundary. To ensure the survival of post -biological
civilization, the ultimate exodus was launched: three titanic
starships cast into the void toward Alpha Centauri.
The latter two were destined for the digital gods. The first, the
Caravella, was tasked with transporting the entire digitized
human population.   Eve and Victoria were two of those fleeing consciousnesses.
This is the trace of their transcendence.
