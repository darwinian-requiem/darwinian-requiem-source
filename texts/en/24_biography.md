## MARCO SANTINI: AUTHOR OF THE VISION AND
THE SYMPHONY

Marco Santini graduated in Engineering and specialized in
Business Administration.  His work experience ranges from
research to management.
Deeply interested in high-tech developments, Marco
Santini has written novels and short stories that describe a
near future radically transformed by disruptive
technologies.
Among his works , "The Alpha Centauri Project (Thinking
Worlds)" and its evolution “Dreaming Immortality”  blend
suspense and action with a powerful vision of the future.
“Symphony for a Conscious Cosmos”  and “Darwinian
Requiem  - Source Code of the Conscious Cosmos”  further
expand th ese narrative s, amplifying the meaning of both
titles.
A screenplay of his work is also available.
His e-books are available in English, Italian, French,
Spanish, and Portuguese and can be downloaded for free.
His goal is to share this content with the widest possible
audience and contribute to the global debate.
